# Health care industry’s big Q1

By [AMANDA CHU](https://www.politico.com/staff/amanda-chu), [DANIEL BARNES](https://www.politico.com/staff/daniel-barnes) and [JACOB WENDLER](https://www.politico.com/staff/jacob-wendler)

04/22/2026 06:00 PM EDT

Presented by

[![](https://static.politico.com/96/d0/9d49f300434b9c21cd7f30de71c6/acslogo.png)](https://www.politico.com/newsletters/politico-influence/2026/04/22/health-care-industrys-big-q1-00887432)

_With help from Daniel Lippman_

The biggest players in the health care industry are pouring record sums into K Street as the Trump administration intensifies scrutiny of their business practices ahead of the midterms, our colleague [Amanda Chu reports](https://www.politico.com/news/2026/04/22/lobbying-health-care-trump-rfk-00886828).

— A look at Q1 lobbying disclosures: **AHIP**, formerly America’s Health Insurance Plans, shelled out $5.3 million in the first three months of 2026, its highest tally for any quarter on record. **PhRMA** spent $12.2 million, the second-highest quarter for the trade association on record. The **American Medical Association** spent nearly $8 million, its second-highest quarter in the past decade.

— Individual companies have also ramped up. U.S. drugmakers **Eli** **Lilly**, **Gilead** and **Regeneron** reported their highest quarterly spending on lobbying on record last quarter. Health insurer **Humana** also reported its highest lobbying spending on record for Q1: $2.6 million.

— The record spending arrives as President **Donald** **Trump** orders Congress to turn his first-year agenda on health care into law. In a sweeping directive announced in January, he told lawmakers to codify his drug pricing deals, send money directly to Americans for health insurance and require more price transparency from insurers. Companies, meanwhile, continue to defend themselves from attacks by Health Secretary **Robert F. Kennedy Jr.** and his Make America Healthy Again movement questioning the safety of ultraprocessed food and drugs.

— **Susan** **Zook**, founder of **Mason Street Consulting**, a health care and technology-focused lobbying shop, expects the high level of activity to stay.

— “I don’t see it slowing down probably until the end of this administration. That’s just not \[Trump’s\] style,” said Zook, who previously served as the health care policy adviser for Sen. **Mike** **Crapo** (R-Idaho), who now chairs the powerful Finance Committee.

— The high lobbying spending from health care companies in the first quarter follows a record year for lobbying by the sector where lobbyists’ efforts largely revolved around the nearly $1 trillion in cuts to Medicaid in Trump’s One Big Beautiful Bill Act and the expiration of enhanced Obamacare subsidies.

— Among the top lobbying shops for pharmaceutical companies in Q1 include **Todd Strategy Group**, **BGR Group** and **Tarplin, Downs & Young**. The top shops for insurers were **Mehlman** **Consulting**, **Continental** **Strategy** and **ALB** **Solutions**.

**GRINDR’S POLITICAL POWER PLAY: Grindr**’s inaugural White House Correspondents Dinner weekend party has Washington abuzz, but the LGBTQ+ dating app’s political ambitions go much deeper, Playbook’s Irie Sentner reports. The company’s lobbying blitz [is being spearheaded by](https://www.politico.com/news/2026/04/22/grindr-washington-trump-congress-00886347) GOP Hill alum and registered Republican **Joe Hack**, who told Irie he’s found room for bipartisan agreement in Washington, even amid the Trump administration’s anti-DEI and LGBTQ+ crackdown.

— Grindr has spent more than $1.6 million on lobbying efforts since it registered to lobby federal lawmakers a year ago, pouring more than $150,000 in Q1 on in-house lobbying and contracts with K Street shops **Brownstein Hyatt Farber Schreck** and **DB3**. The company is focused on pushing priorities like HIV prevention funding, online safety and privacy, according to Hack.

**BAILING OUT SPIRIT:** The Trump administration is [nearing a deal](https://www.wsj.com/business/airlines/trump-administration-nearing-rescue-deal-for-spirit-airlines-2f6a5556) to bail out **Spirit Airlines** in exchange for the federal government taking a “potential significant stake” in the discount carrier, Alison Sider, Brian Schwartz and Andrew Scurria scooped today for The Wall Street Journal.

— Trump reportedly met last night with Commerce Secretary **Howard Lutnick** and Transportation Secretary **Sean Duffy** to discuss the potential deal, which could involve the government lending Spirit as much as $500 million. Such a deal would be a lifeline for Spirit, which has floundered since a judge blocked its potential acquisition by **JetBlue** in 2024 following an antitrust lawsuit filed by the Biden Justice Department.

— The airline has entered bankruptcy twice since then, selling off planes and raising prices. Creditors reportedly explored the possibility of liquidating the company. Now its financial problems are worsening amid skyrocketing jet fuel costs due to the war with Iran.

**TRAVEL INDUSTRY TEAMS UP:** More than 30 travel and tourism industry groups — including **Airlines for America** and the **Electronic Payments Coalition** — are launching the **United States Tourism Economy Alliance**. The group is joining the fierce lobbying fight against Sens. **Dick Durbin**(D-Ill.) and **Roger Marshall**’s (R-Kansas) **Credit Card Competition Act**, which would subject credit card companies to more oversight and could lower costs for merchants who accept cards.

**TECHNET’S LATEST MEMBERS:** The leading association of tech company CEOs and senior executives, **TechNet**, announced three new members today. Telematics manufacturer **Lytx**, autonomous vehicle startup **Waabi**, and Nvidia-backed startup **Reflection**— founded by two former **Google DeepMind** researchers in 2024 — are joining the bipartisan network. TechNet is pushing for a federal AI framework and “an incremental and collaborative approach to AI governance” as it continues to grow.

## flying in

More than 100 retail real estate executives are on the Hill today for the **International Council of Shopping Centers**’ annual fly-in. They’re meeting with bipartisan lawmakers including Sens. **Mitch McConnell**(R-Ky.) and **Tim Sheehy**(R-Mont.) and Reps. **Marilyn Strickland**(D-Wash.), **Greg Landsman**(D-Ohio), **Scott Perry**(R-Pa.) and **Ralph Norman**(R-S.C.). The executives are pushing lawmakers to renew the Terrorism Risk Insurance Program and pass the bipartisan Combatting Organized Retail Crime Act, which aims to help law enforcement crack down on interstate and transnational crime.

… **Meta** is bringing nearly 150 small business owners to the Hill today to discuss with lawmakers how AI and personalized advertising can help drive growth and hiring. The fly-in is part of **Meta Small Business**, an initiative launched by CEO **Mark Zuckerberg** last month to drive AI adoption and support entrepreneurship. They have meetings with Sen. **Angela Alsobrooks** (D-Md.) and staffers of Rep. **Jay Obernolte** (R-Calif.). _(Morning Tech has more on_ [_Big Tech’s recent lobbying spending_](https://subscriber.politicopro.com/newsletter/2026/04/big-tech-lobby-spend-stagnates-ai-startups-surge-00885989) _for Pros)_

… The **Home Care Association of America** hosted its 2026 Advocacy Day today, bringing over 125 members to the Hill to advocate for veteran access to home care, program integrity efforts and tax policy legislation that would ease the financial burden on families. The members have 148 meetings scheduled and heard from Rep. **Mike Carey** (R-Ohio) — a sponsor of the Credit for Caring Act — at their breakfast briefing.

Jacob joined POLITICO as a breaking news intern in 2025, covering everything from the [battle over the future of Young Republicans](https://www.politico.com/news/2025/08/02/maga-future-young-republican-national-convention-00488361) and the [subsequent post-texting scandal meltdown](https://www.politico.com/news/2025/10/19/young-republican-chat-internal-conflict-00614004) to how states are preparing for the [impact of the One Big Beautiful Bill](https://www.politico.com/news/2025/09/27/trump-snap-medicaid-county-cuts-00582624). He has also contributed to POLITICO Nightly, Morning Score and the National Security Daily newsletter.

A New Jersey native, he graduated from Northwestern University — where he served as editor in chief of The Daily Northwestern — with a double major in journalism and political science. Before joining POLITICO, Jacob reported for Bloomberg News and Forbes.
