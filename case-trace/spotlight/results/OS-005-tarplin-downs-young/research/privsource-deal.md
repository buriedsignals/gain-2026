# FGS Global Acquires Tarplin, Downs & Young, LLC

September 10, 2025

FGS Global has acquired Washington, D.C.-based Tarplin, Downs & Young, LLC (TDY), a health care policy and advocacy firm with roughly 20 years of experience advising health clients on legislative and regulatory matters. The acquisition adds bipartisan government affairs and policy expertise to FGS’s health practice, expanding its integrated policy, communications and stakeholder capabilities while keeping the full TDY team intact.
