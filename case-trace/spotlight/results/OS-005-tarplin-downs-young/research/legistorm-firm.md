**Website:** [https://tdyllc.com/](https://tdyllc.com/)

**Organization Description:**

Tarplin, Downs & Young, LLC, opened its doors on January 1, 2006, as a firm specializing in strategic consulting and policy development with a particular focus on health care.

The firm combines the broad political experience and policy expertise of Washington veterans to offer the highest level of service to major corporations and trade associations on their legislative and regulatory priorities. Tarplin, Downs & Young, LLC, is a unique and complementary group in that each partner has worked in the Congress, the Executive branch, and the private sector.
