## A cut above.

* * *

Since its beginning, Capitol Tax Partners has built a diverse, bipartisan tax policy team with high-level experience in all relevant branches of Federal government. The firm’s principals offer our clients an intimate knowledge of the tax legislative and rulemaking process. They have decades of experience in senior positions with the congressional tax-writing committees and the United States Treasury Department, and extensive experience representing private sector clients regarding tax matters before Congress, Treasury, and the Internal Revenue Service.

While in government, members of our team have worked on every tax bill that has become law in the past 30 years, including the Tax Cuts and Jobs Act of 2017, the Protecting Americans from Tax Hikes Act of 2015, and the 2001 and 2003 tax cuts.

No matter your tax issue, Capitol Tax Partners has the expertise to help you develop and execute a strategy that delivers results.
