## Not your ordinary tax policy advocates.

Capitol Tax Partners’ unique blend of technical expertise and legislative experience helps clients navigate the complex tax policymaking process and achieve successful outcomes. Our partners’ broad experience and daily involvement in the process allows us to understand and explain the current climate, quickly and fully assess any situation, and develop creative strategies to accomplish our clients’ desired results.

What separates Capitol Tax Partners is its people. Since 2001, our bipartisan tax policy team has grown to be best-in-class in Washington, including senior government officials with both tax legislative and regulatory experience. This enables us to help steer our clients through the entire process. We are able to provide clients with an honest appraisal of what is likely to happen and then with the best technical and political advice on how best to achieve the desired outcome.
