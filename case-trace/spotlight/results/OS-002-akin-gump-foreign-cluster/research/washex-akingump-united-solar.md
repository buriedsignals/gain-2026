Akin Gump, the [fourth largest](https://www.opensecrets.org/federal-lobbying/top-lobbying-firms) lobbying firm in the country by revenue, is being paid to advocate for a polysilicon manufacturer led by an individual whom the U.S. government has linked to [China’s](https://www.washingtonexaminer.com/tag/china/) repression of the Uyghur people.

In September 2025, Akin Gump entered into a lobbying contract with United Solar Polysilicon to represent its interests on trade policy, according to a [March 31 disclosure](https://lda.gov/filings/public/filing/eda7a0c7-5907-47ce-8581-30bd78fc3424/print/). United Solar Polysilicon is [led](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/united-solar-polysilicon-fzc-spc_ceosummit-omanarabbank-leadership-activity-7392141432679108608-xkfx/) by its [founder](https://www.unitedsolarholding.com/investor), Longgen Zhang.

Zhang was the CEO of Daqo New Energy, another polysilicon manufacturer, from [2018](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/daqo-new-energy-appoints-mr-longgen-zhang-as-chief-executive-officer-300582958.html#:~:text=CHONGQING%2C%20China%2C%20Jan.,Currently%2C%20Mr.) to [2023](https://www.dqsolar.com/2023-08-03-Daqo-New-Energy-Announces-Leadership-Changes#:~:text=He%20has%20been%20a%20highly,SOURCE%20Daqo%20New%20Energy%20Corp.), according to press releases. He also served as the [vice chairman](https://www.yicaiglobal.com/star50news/2024_02_186659216272871391238) of Xinjiang Daqo New Energy, a subsidiary of the company based in China’s Uyghur-inhabited far west, from 2018 to 2022.

During his tenure at Daqo New Energy, the Chinese manufacturing magnate landed in hot water with the federal government multiple times over his links to the Chinese Communist Party’s repression of religious and ethnic minorities. The U.S. government, under both [Republican](https://www.reuters.com/article/world/in-parting-shot-trump-administration-accuses-china-of-genocide-against-uighur-idUSKBN29O25E/) and [Democratic](https://www.axios.com/2021/12/23/biden-signs-uyghur-forced-labor-bill) administrations, as well as [international observers](https://www.humanrightsresearch.org/post/persecution-of-uyghurs-in-xinjiang-torture-crimes-against-humanity-and-genocide) and other [foreign governments](https://uhrp.org/responses/), have described the CCP’s treatment of Uyghurs as a genocide.

In June 2021, for instance, the Department of Commerce [added](https://www.commerce.gov/news/press-releases/2021/06/commerce-department-adds-five-chinese-entities-entity-list) Xinjiang Daqo New Energy to the Bureau of Industry and Security’s Entity List — a catalog of entities that the U.S. government has deemed a risk to its national security or foreign policy interests.
