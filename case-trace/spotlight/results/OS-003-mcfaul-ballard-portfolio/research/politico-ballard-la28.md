# SoftBank hires Miller, Ballard lands ’28 Olympics organizers

By [CAITLIN OPRYSKO](https://www.politico.com/staff/caitlin-oprysko)

02/04/2025 06:16 PM EST

Presented by

[![](https://static.politico.com/96/d0/9d49f300434b9c21cd7f30de71c6/acslogo.png)](https://www.politico.com/newsletters/politico-influence/2025/02/04/softbank-hires-miller-ballard-lands-28-olympics-organizers-00202491)

_With Josh Gerstein, Dana Nickel, Daniel Lippman_

**NEW BUSINESS: Miller Strategies** has registered nine new clients including **SoftBank**, the Japanese tech and media conglomerate whose splashy AI infrastructure investment announcement last month [drew the ire of **Elon Musk**](https://www.politico.com/newsletters/digital-future-daily/2025/01/22/why-trumps-ai-plan-just-caused-a-billionaire-slapfight-00200049).

— GOP fundraiser **Jeff Miller** and **Tyler Grimm,** a former **Jim Jordan** (R-Ohio) aide, began lobbying for SoftBank on AI, technology and energy policy issues beginning on Jan. 1, according to a disclosure filing. That was weeks ahead of a Jan. 21 White House event rolling out the $500 billion investment from SoftBank, **OpenAI** and **Oracle**.

— Miller’s firm also registered to lobby for the **American Hotel & Lodging Association**, investment management company **Capital Group**, the **National Fisheries Institute**, **Finseca**, **NextNav**, crypto exchange **FalconX**, education technology company **Risepoint** and high-speed trading firm **DRW Holdings**.

— Meanwhile, **Ballard Partners** registered yet another new batch of clients, including the organization in charge of planning the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics and Paralympics. **Brian Ballard**, **Dan McFaul**, **Patrick Kilcur** and **Michael LaRosa** will work to foster “intergovernmental agency coordination” on behalf of the organizing committee, according to a disclosure filing.

— A spokesperson for the planning committee who flagged the hire to PI declined to comment on the specifics of Ballard’s work for the organization beyond that it would include federal lobbying. But the contract also comes after committee President **Casey Wasserman** [paid a trip to Mar-a-Lago last month](https://www.axios.com/2025/01/16/trump-los-angeles-2028-olympics) to secure President **Donald Trump**’s support for helping the city recover from the recent devastating wildfires in time for the games. Trump pledged to help make 2028 the “greatest games.”

— The committee also retains **Brownstein Hyatt Farber Schreck** and the **Nickles Group**, while the **U.S. Olympic and Paralympic Committee** retains **Monument Advocacy**. The committee board includes major names like **LA Lakers** owner **Jeanie Buss**, legendary swimmer **Katie Ledecky**, actor **Jessica Alba**, former Trump Transportation Secretary **Elaine Chao** and Hollywood producer (and former **Joe Biden** finance chair) **Jeffrey Katzenberg**.

**FIRST IN PI — PROGRESSIVE THINK TANK STAFFS UP:** Left-leaning economic policy think tank **Groundwork Collaborative** has added a pair of former Biden administration staffers as progressives gear up to try and extract policy wins in this year’s tax fight.

— **Alex Jacquez**, who most recently served as the special assistant to the president for economic development and industrial strategy at the National Economic Council, is joining Groundwork as chief of policy and advocacy, and former Labor Department public engagement chief **Taylor Barnes Lord** will be chief of staff.

— **Liz Pancotti**, who most recently served as director of special initiatives at the **Roosevelt Institute** and is a former **Bernie Sanders** adviser, is joining as managing director of policy and advocacy, while tech and labor policy expert **Katie Wells** will be director of research in addition to her role as a senior fellow.

**MUELLER’S TENTACLES:** **George Nader** — a key witness in special counsel **Robert Mueller**’s investigation of Trump and a large donor to **Hillary Clinton**’s 2016 presidential campaign — has been ordered to give an unusual deposition in a criminal case just days before Nader is to be released from jail and leave the country, **Josh Gerstein** reports.

— However, U.S. District Court Judge **Randolph Moss** [issued the order](https://www.documentcloud.org/documents/25513043-khawajaord020425/) only after delivering a withering rebuke Tuesday to one of the defense lawyers seeking the deposition on behalf of **Andy Khawaja**, a Lebanese American businessperson and prolific political donor facing two criminal prosecutions in the U.S.

— “I don’t think I’ve ever received such an outrageous request in my time as a judge,” Moss told attorney **Kenneth Julian** in response to Khawaja’s request that Nader be blocked from leaving the U.S. until he can testify at a trial that hasn’t been set because Khawaja is a fugitive. “Your request that I should detain Mr. Nader is the greatest non-starter I’ve ever heard,” said Moss, an appointee of President **Barack Obama**. “I would be embarrassed. … It is mind-boggling.”

— Nader is set to be released on Thursday from a halfway house in Baltimore after serving back-to-back prison sentences on child-sex-related charges and a charge related to the foreign-donations scheme where Khawaja remains under indictment. Despite Moss’ evident umbrage, the judge ultimately agreed to require Nader to give a deposition that can be used at a trial for Khawaja, if that ever happens.

— According to prosecutors, [Khawaja and Nader concocted a scheme](https://www.politico.com/news/2019/12/03/george-nader-mueller-indicted-campaign-finance-075046) to funnel millions of dollars from abroad to the U.S. in political donations aimed at currying favor with Clinton on behalf of officials in the United Arab Emirates.

— The men shifted their donations to Republican causes after Clinton’s defeat in the 2016 election, exceeding donation limits and using straw donors to transfer the gifts, prosecutors charged. The alleged link to the UAE caught the attention of Mueller’s investigators as they probed foreign influence on Trump’s orbit during his 2016 presidential bid and transition.

— Khawaja’s gifts appear to have helped him gain [an Oval Office meeting](https://www.nrtoday.com/donor-helping-fraudsters-offshore-bettors-backs-trump/article_75d36891-55b5-5b9c-a722-3738248ae3ea.html) with Trump during his first term in office. Prosecutors said neither the Democratic nor Republican organizations involved were aware of the foreign-donations scheme.

**NONPROFITS TAKE THEIR CASE TO BESSENT:** A coalition of trade associations and other tax-exempt groups looking to stave off any new tax hikes to help pay for Republicans’ tax bill is pleading its case with newly sworn-in Treasury Secretary **Scott Bessent**, [calling in a letter](https://subscriber.politicopro.com/f/?id=00000194-d30c-de45-addf-d3ac325c0000) on Monday for Bessent to “protect and support policies that safeguard and strengthen the nonprofit sector.”

— As our friends over at **Morning Tax** have noted, nonprofits attribute the 2017 tax law with a decline in contributions, and fear Republicans could ding the sector further by taxing part of nonprofits’ earnings at the corporate rate.

— “Increased financial pressures coupled with potential legislative and regulatory changes could threaten nonprofits’ ability to serve Americans,” **Steve Caldeira**, head of the **Household & Commercial Products Association** and co-chair of the **Community Impact Coalition** wrote to Bessent. “Nonprofits could be forced to cut programs, turn people away, or shut their doors entirely,” he added. “That is a cost America cannot afford.”

**EXCLUSIVE CLIMATE FOCUS:** A Democratic policy communications firm is rebranding to exclusively focus on clean energy and climate change, [reports **Timothy Cama** for POLITICO’s E&E News](https://subscriber.politicopro.com/article/eenews/2025/02/04/democratic-communications-firm-turns-toward-climate-00202162).

— **Moyer Strategies**, launched five years ago by **Chris Moyer**, has now rebranded into **Echo Communications** and will completely focus on strategic communications and campaigns for renewable energy, clean technology and climate change clients. Moyer said the firm has previously worked in those spaces.

— “The shift comes as President **Donald Trump** comes to office with Republican congressional majorities and plans to tear down the climate and clean energy policies that former President Joe Biden and Democrats built up.”

— Moyer said the move is a “natural evolution for our firm. Over the past five years, we’ve grown from a one-person consultancy into a dynamic team delivering exceptional results for a growing roster of climate- and renewable energy-focused clients.”

**WAXMAN ESTABLISHES NEW TECH PRACTICE:** **Waxman Strategies** is bringing on Commerce Department alumna **Sarah Morris** as managing director. Morris will develop and lead Waxman’s technology practice.

— “We are excited to welcome Sarah Morris to Waxman Strategies,” CEO **Michael Waxman** said in a statement. “Sarah’s deep expertise and proven leadership in technology policy will be instrumental as we launch this new practice. Her track record of advocating for broadband access, digital privacy, and consumer protections aligns perfectly with our mission to create and sustain change in the public interest.”

— Morris previously served in the Commerce Department as principal deputy assistant secretary at the **National Telecommunications and Information Administration**.

— The technology practice will focus on connecting communities and ensuring technology access by advocating for high-speed and affordable internet services, strengthening data privacy connections and promoting competition in tech and telecommunications sectors.

**MUM’S THE WORD:** Commerce Secretary nominee **Howard Lutnick** “ [won’t say whether he’ll recuse himself](https://www.politico.com/live-updates/2025/02/03/congress/lutnick-crypto-working-group-00202247) from the administration’s cryptocurrency working group despite acknowledging he maintains ties to the controversial crypto firm, **Tether**,” per our **Ben Leonard**.

— “I will follow applicable government ethics laws and regulations based on guidance from the Ethics Office of the Department of Commerce,” Lutnick [said in written responses](https://www.commerce.senate.gov/services/files/01C4706D-6E4A-4869-8785-D9166C796DE7) to a series of questions from Senate Commerce Committee members — including one from the panel’s ranking member, **Maria Cantwell** (D-Wash.), about Trump’s “Working Group on Digital Asset Markets,” which was created to form the first regulatory framework for the crypto industry.

Peral previously was deputy press secretary for the North Carolina coordinated campaign.

— **Marcus Childress** is now special counsel for investigations and senior adviser for the House Judiciary Democrats. He most recently was a partner at **Jenner & Block**, and is a House Jan. 6 committee alum.

— Former acting Labor Secretary **Julie Su** is joining **The Century Foundation** as a senior fellow.

— **Paul Rosen** will be a partner in **Latham & Watkins**’ CFIUS & U.S. national security practice. He most recently was assistant Treasury secretary for investment security, overseeing the Committee on Foreign Investment in the United States.

— **Karly Hahn** is joining **Alamo Intelligence** as director of accounts. She previously was deputy digital director at **Congressional Leadership Fund** for the last two cycles.

— **Perry Spiegel** is now manager for trade at **McLarty Associates**. He previously was a policy adviser for the National Security Council.

— Former Missouri State Sen. **Holly Thompson Rehder** has joined **AxAdvocacy** to lead its government affairs work in Missouri.
